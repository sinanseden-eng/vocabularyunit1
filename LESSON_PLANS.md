# Unit 1 Vocabulary Lesson Plans

> **Coverage principle:** Every class completes Lesson 1 and learns the 11 music words from **beat** through **track**, including **track**. Lessons 2 and 3 are optional consolidation lessons and introduce no new target vocabulary. The abstract nouns and mood/effect adjectives will be taught later in compulsory lessons.

---

**Lesson 1**

**Priority Vocabulary Lesson: Music Vocabulary — Beat to Track**

**Classroom Routines**

**Lesson Procedure**

**1. Video Hook & Listening Purpose (5 minutes)**

- **Activity:** Students watch the short vocabulary video and listen for four musical elements.
- **Instructions:**
  1. T displays the words **beat, rhythm, melody** and **harmony**.
  2. Students write one keyword or short meaning for each word while watching.
  3. T plays the video: [Elements of Music 3 – Beat, Rhythm, Melody, Harmony](https://youtu.be/sGKXHSvqZR0?list=TLGGOC-vio0VKq8wMjA5MjAyNQ).
  4. Students compare their notes with a partner.
  5. T explains that *harmony* is a bridge word and will be studied fully in a later lesson.

**2. Quick Diagnostic (4 minutes)**

- **Activity:** Students match short explanations with vocabulary and show their confidence.
- **Instructions:**
  1. T displays four prompts:
     - A regular musical pulse = ______
     - The main tune = ______
     - The speed of the beat = ______
     - How high or low a sound is = ______
  2. Students write **sure, maybe** or **new** next to each answer.
  3. T quickly checks the answers: **beat, melody, tempo** and **pitch**.

**3. Guided Vocabulary Input & Contrast (12 minutes)**

- **Activity:** Students study all 11 target words on the website.
- **Instructions:**
  1. T opens the **Current Essential Set: beat–track** section.
  2. T presents the complete set: **beat, genre, hit, lyric, melody, pitch, playlist, rhythm, soundtrack, tempo** and **track**.
  3. Students read the definitions and model examples.
  4. T highlights the following distinctions:
     - **beat – rhythm – tempo**
     - **melody – pitch**
     - **track – hit – soundtrack**
     - **genre – playlist**
  5. Students give a quick example for selected words.

**4. Selective Personal Associations (7 minutes)**

- **Activity:** Students create memorable connections for three words.
- **Instructions:**
  1. Students choose three target words they want to remember better.
  2. They enter one personal association for each word on the website.
  3. Students explain two associations using:
     - **“______ makes me think of ______ because ______.”**

**5. Examples, Collocations & Production (8 minutes)**

- **Activity:** Students notice natural word partnerships and create sentences.
- **Instructions:**
  1. Students reveal model examples for selected words.
  2. T highlights useful chunks such as **a steady beat, a catchy melody, a fast tempo, meaningful lyrics, create a playlist** and **a track on an album**.
  3. Students write two original sentences using two different target words and their natural partners.
  4. Partners check meaning and collocation.

**6. Quick Recall & Exit Check (4 minutes)**

- **Activity:** Students retrieve vocabulary without looking at the complete cards.
- **Instructions:**
  1. T opens **Music Quick Recall**.
  2. Students recall six words from images, definitions or collocations.
  3. Each student writes one distinction they can now explain, such as **beat versus rhythm**.

**Total: 40 minutes**

**Lesson 2**

**Optional Consolidation: Connection Lab & Playlist Challenge**

**Classroom Routines**

**Lesson Procedure**

**1. Retrieval Relay (5 minutes)**

- **Activity:** Teams recall the same 11 music words from short clues.
- **Instructions:**
  1. T displays six definitions or images.
  2. Teams write the corresponding words.
  3. T checks answers without reteaching the definitions.

**2. Association Deck (8 minutes)**

- **Activity:** Students strengthen personal memory links.
- **Instructions:**
  1. T opens the **Music Association Deck**.
  2. Students choose one word from each of these groups:
     - musical elements,
     - music types,
     - songs and collections.
  3. Students create and explain three associations.

**3. Contrast & Collocation Relay (8 minutes)**

- **Activity:** Teams match words with their natural partners and correct confusing combinations.
- **Instructions:**
  1. T gives teams word and collocation cards.
  2. Students form combinations such as **catchy melody, steady beat, meaningful lyrics, film soundtrack, title track** and **fast tempo**.
  3. Teams explain two easily confused words.

**4. Playlist Curator Challenge (14 minutes)**

- **Activity:** Groups design a three-part playlist for different purposes.
- **Instructions:**
  1. Groups create playlist sections for **focus, energy** and **reflection**.
  2. They describe their choices using at least six target words.
  3. Groups prepare a one-minute presentation.
  4. The class selects the most convincing playlist.

**5. Exit Ticket (5 minutes)**

- **Activity:** Students record evidence of deeper learning.
- **Instructions:**
  1. Students write one new collocation they can use confidently.
  2. They write one target word they still need to review.
  3. T uses the responses to identify words requiring later recycling.

**Total: 40 minutes**

**Lesson 3**

**Optional Consolidation: Vocabulary Mixing Desk**

**Classroom Routines**

**Lesson Procedure**

**1. Quick Recall Warm-Up (5 minutes)**

- **Activity:** Students retrieve six words independently.
- **Instructions:**
  1. T opens **Music Quick Recall**.
  2. Students write their answers before each card is revealed.
  3. Students correct their own answers.

**2. Definition Taboo (8 minutes)**

- **Activity:** Students describe target words without saying them.
- **Instructions:**
  1. One student describes a word to their group.
  2. The speaker cannot use the target word or its direct synonym.
  3. The group guesses and uses the word in a short phrase.

**3. Error-Repair Challenge (8 minutes)**

- **Activity:** Teams correct inaccurate meanings and collocations.
- **Instructions:**
  1. T displays sentences containing errors such as:
     - *dance with the beat*
     - *add a song in my playlist*
     - *a hit music*
     - *the soundtrack in the film*
  2. Teams correct the sentences and explain their choices.

**4. Soundtrack Consultant Pitch (14 minutes)**

- **Activity:** Groups recommend music for a film or game scene.
- **Instructions:**
  1. Groups choose a scene such as a chase, celebration, mystery or emotional ending.
  2. They select an appropriate track or genre.
  3. Groups prepare a one-minute pitch using at least six target words.
  4. Listeners vote for the proposal that uses the vocabulary most accurately and persuasively.

**5. Final Recall & Reflection (5 minutes)**

- **Activity:** Students demonstrate individual retention.
- **Instructions:**
  1. Students write as many of the 11 target words as they can.
  2. They add one correct collocation for three of the words.
  3. Students identify one word they will review independently.

**Total: 40 minutes**
